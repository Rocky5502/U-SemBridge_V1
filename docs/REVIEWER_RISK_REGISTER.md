# Reviewer Risk Register

1. **"This is just LLM + solver."** Response: novelty must come from semantic-risk modeling, CIR, calibrated selective action, and evidence that these catch formally valid semantic errors.
2. **"CIR is only engineering."** Response: test its measurable effect with ablations, expert correction, and semantic fidelity metrics.
3. **"Uncertainty is heuristic."** Response: calibrate against observed semantic errors and report Brier/ECE/AUROC/risk-coverage.
4. **"Human in the loop is vague."** Response: define exact intervention points and do not claim benefit without a study.
5. **"Fuzzy logic is bolted on."** Response: keep fuzzy/graded predicate experiments optional and separate from epistemic translation uncertainty.
6. **"Benchmarks may be contaminated."** Response: paired comparisons, frozen prompts, no test demonstrations, explicit limitation.
7. **"Legal claims are unsafe."** Response: no legal-advice claims; quantitative legal evaluation only with expert gold formalizations.
